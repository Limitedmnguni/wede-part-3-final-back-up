Our History & Craft

About Precision Auto PMB

Founded at 25 Melville Road in Pelham, Pietermaritzburg, Precision Auto Performance & Audio was established to serve local automotive enthusiasts with uncompromising standards in aftermarket audio integration, stance tuning and precision auto electrical installations.

Technical Excellence Without Compromise

Whether tuning high-power monoblock amplifier outputs, resolving parasitic battery draw, or setting up custom stance suspension and wheel fitments, our Pelham workshop approaches every vehicle with technical rigor and detail.

We take pride in clean wire management, proper fuse sizing, robust grounding practices and meticulous vehicle care across every installation.

Electrical Integrity
