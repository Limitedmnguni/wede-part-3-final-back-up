About Precision Auto PMB

Since 2021, our team has built a reputation for expertly balancing performance styling, precision fitment, and dependable automotive electronics for local drivers around Pietermaritzburg.

Company Background & History

Operating from 928 Victoria Road in Pietermaritzburg, Precision Auto Performance & Audio has grown into a leading independent automotive fitment and custom audio centre serving the KZN Midlands region.

Our workshop was founded to deliver professional-grade stance modifications, exact wheel fitments, and high-performance audio tuning with technical precision and road-safe workmanship.

Mission & Vision

A local workshop built on craftsmanship, safety, and trust.

Mission Statement

Precision Auto Performance & Audio aims to deliver high-tier automotive aesthetic, acoustic, and fitment upgrades to the Pietermaritzburg motoring community through specialized local technical craftsmanship.

Vision Statement

We aim to become the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands region's preferred automotive customization workshop by setting standards for reliability, safety compliance, and cutting-edge audio engineering.
