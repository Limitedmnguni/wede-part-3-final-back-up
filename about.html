Company Background & History

Operating since 2021 from 928 Victoria Road in Pietermaritzburg, Precision Auto Performance & Audio has grown into a leading independent automotive fitment and custom audio centre in the KZN Midlands region.

Our workshop was founded to provide vehicle owners with professional-grade stance modifications, exact wheel fitments and high-performance car audio tuning built on technical accuracy and safety standards.

Mission & Vision

Mission Statement: Precision Auto Performance & Audio aims to deliver high-tier automotive aesthetic, acoustic and fitment upgrades to the Pietermaritzburg motoring community through specialized local technical craftsmanship.

Vision Statement: The business envisions becoming the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands region's preferred automotive customization workshop by setting industry standards for reliability, safety compliance and cutting-edge audio engineering.
